## 10 Sample JAMB-Style Questions with Full Solutions

### Question 1
Simplify: log₁₀ 25 + log₁₀ 4

**Answer: 2**

**Solution:** Using the log product rule: log₁₀(25 × 4) = log₁₀(100) = log₁₀(10²) = 2

---

### Question 2
Find the value of x if 2^(x+1) = 32

**Answer: x = 4**

**Solution:** 32 = 2⁵, so 2^(x+1) = 2⁵, which means x+1 = 5, therefore x = 4

---

### Question 3
The sum of the first n terms of an AP is 3n² + 5n. Find the common difference.

**Answer: 6**

**Solution:** The nth term Tₙ = Sₙ – S(n–1) = (3n²+5n) – [3(n–1)²+5(n–1)] = 6n + 2. The common difference d = Tₙ – T(n–1) = 6.

---

### Question 4
Solve for x: 3x² – 7x + 2 = 0

**Answer: x = 2 or x = 1/3**

**Solution:** Factorising: (3x – 1)(x – 2) = 0, so x = 1/3 or x = 2

---

### Question 5
A bearing of 240° is equivalent to what compass direction?

**Answer: S60°W**

**Solution:** 240° is in the third quadrant (SW). It is 240° – 180° = 60° past South towards West, so the compass notation is S60°W.

---

### Question 6
Evaluate the integral of (3x² + 2x) from 0 to 2.

**Answer: 12**

**Solution:** Integrating: [x³ + x²] from 0 to 2 = (8 + 4) – (0 + 0) = 12

---

### Question 7
The mean of five numbers is 6. Four of the numbers are 4, 8, 3, and 7. Find the fifth number.

**Answer: 8**

**Solution:** Sum = 5 × 6 = 30. Known sum = 4+8+3+7 = 22. Fifth number = 30 – 22 = 8.

---

### Question 8
Simplify √3 ÷ (√3 – 1), rationalising the denominator.

**Answer: (3 + √3) ÷ 2**

**Solution:** Multiply numerator and denominator by (√3 + 1): [√3(√3 + 1)] ÷ [(√3)² – 1²] = (3 + √3) ÷ 2

---

### Question 9
In how many ways can the letters of the word JAMB be arranged?

**Answer: 24**

**Solution:** JAMB has 4 distinct letters. Arrangements = 4! = 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24

---

### Question 10
A die is thrown twice. What is the probability that the sum of the two outcomes is 9?

**Answer: 1/9**

**Solution:** Favourable outcomes: (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3) — 4 outcomes. Total outcomes = 6 × 6 = 36. Probability = 4/36 = 1/9

## Study Tips Specific to JAMB Mathematics

**1. Start with Algebra.** It has the highest weighting. Master factorisation, quadratic equations, and logarithm laws before anything else.

**2. Know your calculator shortcuts.** JAMB CBT has a built-in calculator. Practice using it efficiently so you are not wasting time on arithmetic during the exam.

**3. Time yourself.** JAMB gives you 40 minutes for 40 Mathematics questions — exactly 1 minute per question. Train under that constraint from day one.

**4. Do not skip Statistics.** Mean, median, mode, probability — these are reliably easy marks. Never leave them out of your revision.

**5. Sketch geometry problems.** A quick hand-drawn diagram before calculating saves you from picking the wrong answer option.

**6. Check with substitution.** For multiple-choice algebra questions, substitute the answer options back into the equation. It is often faster than solving from scratch.
